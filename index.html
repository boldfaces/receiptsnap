Sonnet reads dates and faded thermal receipts far more reliably. Haiku is cheaper per scan if quality holds for your receipts.

Storage location

PDFs are saved into monthly subfolders, e.g. /Receipts/2026-07/. Claims.xlsx sits in the base folder. Change only before your first batch.
Shown on other devices next to receipts scanned here, e.g. "Alexander's phone".

Entities

Which company each receipt belongs to, comma-separated. With more than one, every receipt must be assigned before filing; History and the chart can filter by entity.

Tax treatment hints

One line per purpose, "Purpose = Treatment". Written into the Tax Treatment column as a hint for your accountant. These defaults are common Malaysian practice, not advice: confirm them with your accountant once, then they apply to every receipt.

Purpose labels

One-tap labels on the review screen, comma-separated. "Others" is always added at the end with a free-text field.

Danger zone

Erase every scanned receipt: deletes the whole receipts folder in OneDrive (all PDFs and Claims.xlsx), the history cache on this phone, and any receipts in the batch tray. Sign-in, API key and labels are kept.
